Every club leaves a bench, not a production line

We are, as far as the trade knows, the only golf club manufacturer in the world still retaining the traditional tools and skills of the original Scottish makers. Clubs are made to order, by hand, on the premises.

That means a hickory set can be built for period play, and a modern club can be made to meet the current Rules of Golf, all from the same workshop in Fife.

  • Handcrafted hickory clubs in the traditional manner
  • Modern clubs built to meet R&A Rules of Golf
  • Personalised commissions and corporate prizes, made to order
  • Sourcing, authentication and restoration of antique clubs
