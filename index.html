Questions, Answered

What people ask before they enquire

What do you make?

We make hickory and modern golf clubs, built to order by hand on the premises in Fife. Alongside the clubs we offer wooden head putters including our Stag model, golf balls and accessories, and the sourcing, authentication and restoration of antique clubs.

Are you really the last Scottish clubmaker?

As far as the trade knows, we are the only golf club manufacturer in the world still retaining the traditional tools and skills of the original Scottish makers, and the last remaining manufacturer of golf clubs in Scotland. Every club leaves a working bench in St Andrews rather than a production line.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. We ship internationally and work with hickory players, societies and collectors in the United States, Japan, across Europe and beyond. We also work with partners and sales agents in newer markets, so if you run a club or a collection abroad we are glad to talk it through.

How does the made to order and fitting process work?

Clubs are built around the player and the brief. You tell us how you intend to use them, whether that is period hickory play or a modern set, and we make the clubs to order by hand. We also handle personalised commissions and corporate prizes. Start by sending us an enquiry and we will talk through the detail with you.

Do you repair or re-shaft clubs?

Yes. We carry out repairs, re-shafting and care for treasured clubs, using the same traditional tools the trade has relied on for generations. If you have a club you would like looked at, send us a note describing it and we will tell you what is possible.

What is the history?

The lineage we carry traces to the George Nicoll name from 1881, rooted in Leven, and to Tom Stewart of St Andrews from 1904, a maker to leading players of the era. St Andrews Golf Company carries these historic Scottish clubmakers forward under one roof in the Home of Golf.

Where are you based?

We have a factory at 20 St Andrews Road, Largoward, by St Andrews, Fife, KY9 1HZ, and a shop at 8 Golf Place, St Andrews, Fife, KY16 9JA. You will find a map and both telephone numbers in the section below.
